The details

Everything is made in small batches on the day. The cookie base for the tarts is rolled thin so it stays crisp under the cream. The bomboloni are brioche, fried first thing in the morning, and they are gone by the afternoon.

We are a two-person kitchen, which is why we ask for notice. Saturdays fill up about a week out, so order early for weekends.

Questions

Anything else, text us. We answer between bakes.

How far ahead do I need to order?
Bomboloni and kunafa cups need a day. Cream tarts (numbers, letters, hearts, rings) need two days. Vintage and name cakes need three. The checkout only lets you pick dates that work for what is in your box.
Can I change my order after paying?
Yes, up to 48 hours before your pickup. Text us the order number and what you want changed. Inside 48 hours the cookie bases are already made, so we can only swap toppings.
Do you deliver?
Within 15 miles, for $12, free when your order is over $150. Someone needs to be home to take the box. Everything travels upright and chilled.
What is the cancellation policy?
Full refund with 72 hours' notice, half with 48, none inside 48 since by then it is baked. Weather and emergencies: text us, we are reasonable people.
Can I bring my own candies or topper?
Yes. Drop them off the day before pickup, sealed, and tell us in the notes at checkout. We will build around them.
Are the toppers edible?
The acrylic Happy Birthday toppers, numbers and crowns are keepsakes, not food. Everything else on the cake, including the gold almonds and the mini chocolate bars, is edible.
